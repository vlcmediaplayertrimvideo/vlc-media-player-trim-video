💡 VLC Trim Tip

VLC does not provide a traditional timeline trimming editor. A common method is to use its Record control to capture the desired section as a new video clip.

❓ Quick FAQ

Can VLC Media Player trim a video?

VLC can create a shorter clip by recording the desired portion during playback. It is different from a traditional timeline-based video editor.

Where are Advanced Controls in VLC?

On the Windows desktop interface, open the View menu and enable Advanced Controls.

Can VLC play the trimmed clip?

Yes. The resulting supported media file can be opened and played in VLC.
